# WiseWay

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
delegated: Java 21 + Spring Boot REST API, SQLite/JPA persistence, and React/Vite frontend.

## Users
Everyday commuters comparing a repeat journey, tracking actual spend, or recovering from a changed travel plan.

## Product Purpose
WiseWay helps commuters compare illustrative transport options, save recurring routes, track actual commute expenses, and find an explainable Plan B when time or budget changes.

## Positioning
Plan B recovery plus preference-aware, deterministic recommendations that show the trade-off instead of pretending one route is universally best.

## Operating Context
Single-user local demo. No account, paid API, live transit feed, GPS tracking, ticketing, or external AI service is required.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The application supports journey comparison, Budget Saver, Time Saver, Comfort First and Balanced profiles, saved-route CRUD, expense logging/summaries, and simulated Plan B scenarios. All transport fares and durations are sample estimates, not live information. Currency is INR.

## Brand Commitments
Product name: WiseWay. Tagline: “Plan your route. Stay ready.”

## Evidence on Hand
The repository includes the original product requirements and design documents, but no external customer research or live transport data.

## Product Principles
- Be honest about data quality.
- Filter hard constraints before ranking.
- Explain recommendations using returned fields.
- Keep the local MVP small and free to run.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use semantic controls, visible focus, labels, keyboard operation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and non-colour-only status communication.
